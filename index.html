06 / FAQ

The operational details.

How do we send you a brief?

Through WhatsApp or Notion — whichever fits your team's workflow.

What's your delivery capacity?

Up to 3 high-end motion graphics and 5 basic ad videos — every single day.

How does pricing work?

Per-project — every job gets a mutually agreed quote before work starts. No retainers, no minimums.

What if we're not happy with a delivery?

We rework it. Revision requests are accepted up to 3 days after the first draft is delivered.

Are you really white-label?

Yes. Your name goes on the work, and we never contact your clients — we're a studio, not a competing agency.

Can you handle bulk volumes?

That's what the pipeline is built for — specialized creatives per format plus a QC layer that doesn't slip when volume grows.

Who owns the files?

The final deliverables are fully yours. The working project files stay with Design Dynamo.
